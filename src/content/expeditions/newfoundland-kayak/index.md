I led a 49-day sea kayak expedition around the island of Newfoundland with Jack Kotaska, covering more than 2,200 km. I was responsible for route planning, risk assessment, safety protocols, and finding sponsors.

## In the news

- **CBC News:** [Chasing a record, at the whims of the sea, 2 kayakers paddle around Newfoundland in 49 days](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/newfoundland-labrador/aroundtherock-ocean-kayak-1.7302040) (August 27, 2024)
- **Sea Kayaking UK:** [Around the Rock Expedition](https://seakayakinguk.com/expedition-eports/around-the-rock-expedition)
- **Polite Canada (Facebook):** [Two kayakers, Newfoundlander David MacKay and Jack Kotaska…](https://www.facebook.com/politecanada/posts/two-kayakers-newfoundlander-david-mackay-and-jack-kotaska-from-the-northwest-ter/122163552770202709/)
