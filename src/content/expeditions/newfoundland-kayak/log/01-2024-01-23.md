Welcome to “Around the Rock”, a two person sea kayak expedition to circumnavigate the island of Newfoundland. With the goal of beating the current record of 44 days, documenting the expedition, and celebrating Newfoundland’s history and people through video, interviews, and written media.

Keep an eye on this page and our website for updates on our trip as we prepare and plan… There is lots to do, but with any luck we will set out from Quidi Vidi this june! - David
