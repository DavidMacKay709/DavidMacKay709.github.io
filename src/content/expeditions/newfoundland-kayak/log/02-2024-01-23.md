**Hello! I’m David,**

It was family trips that introduced me to sea kayaking, revealing an extraordinary way to explore Newfoundland’s rugged coastline. Since then I’ve paddled whitewater rivers across Newfoundland and ventured into other parts of Canada. This new passion led me to become a sea kayak guide on the southern shore, immersing myself in the beauty of my home province.

The summer of 2020 was when the idea of circumnavigating Newfoundland first took. My kayaking has taken me far and wide. This past summer I competed in the Yukon River Quest, a  715 km race from Whitehorse to Dawson City, and spent nine days solo in Alaska, marveling at the stunning fjords and glaciers.

Each trip, always brought my thoughts back to Newfoundland, to the challenge of paddling around ‘The Rock’. Now, as I wrap up my final semester of my degree, the timing feels right to embark on this quest. I’m thrilled at the prospect of what lies ahead and eager to discover what Newfoundland has in store for me!
