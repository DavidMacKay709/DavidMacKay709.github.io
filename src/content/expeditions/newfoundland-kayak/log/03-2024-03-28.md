Hello, my name is Jack and I’m stoked to be paddling around the Island of Newfoundland with [@davemac709](https://www.instagram.com/davemac709/) this summer. \
Nowadays, I am taking English and History at the University of Victoria and enjoying the great whitewater/ocean kayaking, kite surfing, running, and soccer Vancouver Island offers. However, the events that led me to embark on this trip of a lifetime occurred in my hometown of Yellowknife, Northwest Territories. Growing up in Yellowknife, I explored its many lakes and rivers endlessly with my family. Over time, what started as short canoe trips eventually advanced to multi-week trips throughout the Tlicho, then to spending summers kayaking on the Slave River, and now for the past four years has turned into guiding Jackpine Paddle wilderness canoe and kayak trips throughout the Northwest Territories. \
Newfoundland has always been a fascinating place to me. Although this will be my first time on the East Coast, its wonderful people, outstanding music, and breathtaking landscapes fill me with anticipation and gratitude for the voyage ahead of us. As Around the Rock 2024 approaches, I am looking forward to experiencing Newfoundland’s rugged coastlines, vast oceans, and unique culture.
