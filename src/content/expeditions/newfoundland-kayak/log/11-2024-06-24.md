**Day 3: Cape Broyle ➡️ Chance Cove, 55km**

We got on the water after an early morning dip and made good speed after leaving Admirals Cove.

Passing by Ferryland and Capahayden, it was cool to see the landscape change as we came down to the southern Avalon peninsula. The trees thinned out and the coastline became more barren.

The end of the day was tough and we faced strong headwinds and big swell as we rounded Cape Ballard. Following a good slog we found a sheltered beach in Chance Cove to land and camp.

Quite exhausted, we had a scoff of spam and ramen noodles before turning in early.
