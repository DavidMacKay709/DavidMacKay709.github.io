**Day 4: Rest day in Chance Cove**

Strong SW winds in the morning wouldn’t allow for paddling around Cape Race so we opted to take a rest and enjoy the heat.

We swam in the ocean throughout the day to cool down and had to make a trek to gather some freshwater. The heat was glorious and we stoped to enjoy multiple naps along the way.

Jack’s Haiku

Sound sleep, strait to swim \
Napped, then went to find water \
Bugs, bush, barachois

Fun fact, a barachois is a term used in Atlantic Canada to describe a coastal lagoon that is partially separated from the ocean by a sand or shingle bar. This one was fed by a river and supplemented by some aqua tabs, served as our source of fresh water for the day.
