**Day 7-11 Compilation**

We’ve covered a fair bit of coastline in the past couple days…

Day 7 brought us to a well decorated porch (pictured) in St Brides where we had good enough service to check the weather. The report did not favour a crossing for the next lil bit so we declared day 8 a rest day.

On the 8th day we rested.

Day 9 had been looming over us like a dark cloud for some time and the Oilers loss had further harshened our mellow. However, the day was in fact not cloudy but foggy and Placentia bay was not expecting any big ship traffic according to the coast guard so we set off into the mist. The road was long but the NE wind was ample and we made it to Burin 12 hours and 80km later, just before dark.

Day 10 was predicted to have better conditions for traversing the Burin Peninsula than the days that followed so we decided to push forward. Tired and sore, we slept in then crushed the 60km to Lamaline.

Day 11 was today and we spent it eating, gathering food, and writing haikus.
