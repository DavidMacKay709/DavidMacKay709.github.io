The morning of Day 12 started and remained cold and wet and foggy! We paddled from Lamaline and camped on Brunette Island (60km), the tail wind we had hoped for unfortunately didn’t manifest which led to slow paddling conditions.

It wasn’t all gloom and doom, the weather cleared the next morning and we woke up to the sun and dry gear.

Attempting to paddle to Francois on Day 13, again our plans were thwarted. Headwinds and swell picked up after about 30km and we opted to pull into Pass Island for a total distance of around 40km on the day.

Pass Island is a beautiful island near Hermitage on the Connaigre Peninsula. It was resettled in 1974, the last community to move under the resettlement program.

Currently there is a coast guard base there. We were greeted by the operators Charles and Conrad who maintain this station; working 28 days on, 28 days off. Sleeping on a pull out couch, we were grateful for a warm bed, delicious homemade cake, and great conversation that their hospitality provided!
