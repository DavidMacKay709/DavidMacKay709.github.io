**Progress Report and Day 14&15 on the South Coast**

Following a great day of extra curricular activities in Francois, we are continuing our journey along the South Coast feeling rejuvenated.

This map is marked with our past campsites and gives some perspective to the distance we’ve covered in our first 2 weeks of paddling, around 500km + or - ;) of the total 2000km.

We are looking forward to the next 500km which will largely be on the West Coast where predominantly SW winds and swell promises good paddling conditions.

While in Francois, we enjoyed a warm place to stay, home cooked meals, glorious hiking trails and some crabbing. We are grateful for the incredible hospitality we received here.
