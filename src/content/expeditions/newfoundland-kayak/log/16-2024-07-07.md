**We’ve lost track of the days which puts the record easily within reach.**

After setting out from Francois, we paddled a rugged section of coast to Grey River (40km) and stopped at Cul de Sac West along the way (pic1). We had lunch here and came across a number of marble headstones that dated back to the 1800s.

We spent the night in Grey River then picked up some treats from the store on our morning walk and set out for Burgeo in the sun (45km). On our paddle, we encountered a bunch of porpoises that swam between us as we went. In Burgeo we were greeted warmly by our friends Kathleen and Robert who used to live in Yellowknife. They spoiled us and made 15 hours feel like a whole rest day.

We had great weather and calm water the next day and made it 60km to Jaques Island near La Poile.

Rain rolled in and it was overcast for our 65km paddle to Port au Basques but we were helped along with a slight tailwind.

Sean came in clutch with a resupply in the am  before we set out to cover the 50km to Codroy. It was overcast but the wind was at our backs and we enjoyed surfing alongside a pod of fin whales along the way.

Slept in a cosy fish plant and are now gearing up to tackle the Port au Port Peninsula.

It appears those are days 16-20:)
