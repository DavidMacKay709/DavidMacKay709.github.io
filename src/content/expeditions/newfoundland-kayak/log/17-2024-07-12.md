We like the West Coast \
It has good wind and water \
Off to the races

Day 21: the weather was not ideal for our crossing to the Port au Port Peninsula so we spent the day exploring the Codroy Valley. It was a good choice to stay put as we enjoyed tea with a lovely couple from Ontario and their grandson and got to eat at a diner with our friend Cody. Robin and Brian generously hosted us in their shed for the night so we were well rested for our big paddle the next morning.

Day 22: We enjoyed morning coffee and date squares before reluctantly leaving Codroy in the light fog. The rest of the day was a sunny/starry blur as we slogged 80km across St Georges Bay.

Day 23: That morning our short sleep on the wharf was cut short by the sound of a bulldozer unloading freshly caught halibut. It was too hot to fall back sleep so we hit the water. We fought a headwind until we rounded Cape St George and it became a decent tailwind. That night we camped on the beach near Winterhouse (40km), just before The Bar (a 15km strip of land only a couple hundred meters wide that separates Port au Port Bay from the Gulf of St Lawrence). Some b’ys met us there with beers 🍻.

Day 24: Mostly overcast paddle across Port au Port Bay and along the coast to Little Port (65km). The fog broke briefly as we passed Shag Island and we caught a glimpse of the Lewis Hills (highest point in NFLD) shooting up into the clouds.

Day 25: Slight following sea and a bluebird day helped us along to Rocky Harbour (70km). The long day payed off and that night we indulged in some properly prepared meals and the comfort of a hotel room.
