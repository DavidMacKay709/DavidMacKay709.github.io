**Progress update: We’re halfway there!**

We’ve covered approximately 1050km. The past three weeks have been epic. The support we’ve received thus far has been amazing and motivates us to keep pushing!

It been cool to see the landscape change as we paddle, from the South Coast’s Avalon and Burin peninsulas to the more sheltered waters of the West Coast. As we approach the Great Northern Peninsula, we are encountering calmer waters and coastlines of towering mountains.

We couldn’t be happier with our gear, the boats from [@seakayakinguk](https://www.instagram.com/seakayakinguk/) have been handling the rough conditions well and track nicely in big water. They’re even comfortable enough to sit in for 16 hours! Our Celtic Paddles are extremely light and help reduce the stress of paddling on our shoulders and arms, although we are still quite sore!

Now we keep pushing as we continue to grind for the second half of the trip! Thanks again for all the support and well wishes.
