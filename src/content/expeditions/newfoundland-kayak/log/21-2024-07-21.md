Welcome to Ship Cove \
Warm dinner, warm shed, wide smiles \
Tomorrow we sing

Day 32: Shortly after arriving in Ship Cove, we met some wonderful people who lifted our spirits considerably. Kevin offered for us to use his shed and invited us inside for bottled moose and tea. Before we had a chance to walk over, the Tuckers next door asked if we had heard about the Blue Rodeo concert the next night and if we would like their 2 extra tickets. We gratefully accepted. The shed had an epic wood stove and was a cosy place to sleep and dry clothes.

Day 33: The sun was shining and our clothes were dry as we made the short crossing over to L’anse aux Meadows, the site of a thousand year old Norse settlement and a UNESCO world heritage centre. We took notes as we walked around the grass huts then filled up on some delicious pies from Emily’s Jams and Crafts before continuing our paddle down to the venue in Gunner’s Cove. The offshore wind was strong but our motivation was stronger and we arrived at our prettifull wharf campsite with the sound of Shanneyganock echoing throughout the bay. 25km
