The weather on the East Coast of the Great Northern Peninsula has been slightly frustrating.

Although we had originally planned on paddling across to the Groais Islands, winds forced us to continue Southwest down the peninsula.

After picking up a resupply in St Anthony, we headed for Fischot island, an old resettled community with an interesting history. And we got to see some caribou!

Fischot island, was mainly used by the French to dock during the fishing season, it was attacked by the British during the Newfoundland campaign, at the onset of King George’s war (1744).

We then headed for Grandois. We slept in an abandoned fish plant, last used in 1992, the year the moratorium took effect. A big wind forced us to take a day off here.

We are now in Englee, a beautiful town with what seems to be an active fishing industry, a couple shops, and a place to grab a cup of coffee this morning!
