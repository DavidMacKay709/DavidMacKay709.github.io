Days 38-40 were good. We’ve seen some gorgeous sections of coastline, had mostly sunny weather, and met many nice people.

Day 38: We filled up on gas and snacks in Englee then continued our trek southward along the Northern Peninsula. It was hot but a strong headwind kept us cool. Fjords make up much of the coast here and tend to funnel the wind, we had to cross a bunch of them so weren’t moving very fast. We found a nice one to camp in and had a fire on the bouldery beach.

Day 39: We woke up at 4:30 to catch the decent wind and made good time on our 45km crossing to the Horse Islands. Where we landed was well protected by cool rock formations and made for pristine swimming and cliff jumping. We hung out in the sun for a while then set out into a headwind for La Scie (35km). We arrived in its protected harbour 6 hours later, after dark.

Day 40: Our next leg of paddling is an 80km crossing to Twillingate and the weather was stormy today (unsuitable) so we got to enjoy a day in La Scie. After sleeping in we met a friendly fisherman who lent us his truck and let us stay in his stage, out of the rain. We drove into town for a delicious meal at the museum/tea house (highly recommended) and got to learn about the history of the area.
