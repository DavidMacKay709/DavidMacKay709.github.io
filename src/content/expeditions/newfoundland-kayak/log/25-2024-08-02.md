Day 41: We packed up our things from the fishing stage we stayed in the night before and got an early start for our crossing of Notre Dame Bay to Twillingate (72km)

As we rounded Cape St John we passed Gull Island. While staying in La Scie we learned about a ship wreck here during the 1700s where all 15 of the crew died. The tragic story is similar to the song “Tiny Red Light” by the Dorymen.

That evening we arrived in Twilingate and were greeted by Amy, a local sea kayak guide. Her hospitality was amazing, and we were treated to dinner, beer, and a place to sleep. The next morning Amy brought us on a tour of Twilingate and took us grocery shopping.

Day 42: We got off late after a relaxing morning and made our way towards Joe Batts Arm on Fogo Island (50 km)

We had the wind at our backs and made good time, enjoying the warm weather. On route we saw lots of whales and the first big shark of the trip, it might have been 12 feet long!

Wondering weather or not we should be scared, we continued our paddle and enjoyed a lovely reception from some of Jacks family friends, and other Fogo islanders.

Day 43: It was a wind day in Joe Bats Arm, and we couldn’t have picked a better place to be wind bound. We spent the day exploring the island, swimming, eating, and learning about the area. Huge thank you to the Deckers!
