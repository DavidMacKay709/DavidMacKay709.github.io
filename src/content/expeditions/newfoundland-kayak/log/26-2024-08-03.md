Day 44 (30.07.2024): Fogo-Lumsden (55km) Aha, the day of the record. Greg Stammers 2008 44 day Circumnavigation of Newfoundland helped motivate us at many points throughout the trip. At the start it didn’t seem possible at all but when we had good conditions up the west coast and our fitness had improved, it started to seem feasible. We’ve since encountered many challenges (mostly in the form of wind) and it seems to have evaded us. However, we are not disheartened! We’re pleasantly surprised every time we look at the map now and feel fortunate to have enjoyed such beautiful coastline and even more beautiful people (getting to know folks along the way has been the highlight of the trip).

Lumsden is about 200km from St Johns but we’re waaaaay past that now:)
