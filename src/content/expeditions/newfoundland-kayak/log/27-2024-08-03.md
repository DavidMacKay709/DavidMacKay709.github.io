Day 45: Lumsden - Cape Freals (25km) We paddled in the blistering heat past some amazing beaches and were then graced by a heavy rain as we pulled in to Cape Freals North. We posted up in a harbour authority building awaiting a suitable weather window to cross Bonavista Bay.

Day 46: Cape Freals - Bonavista (80km) Our window came at 2am and we were off. We paddled in the dark for a couple hours then enjoyed the sunrise. Unfortunately, the heat of the day came on around 11 and made the last 4 hours of paddling quite miserable. Thankfully, when we arrived in Bonavista, we stumbled across a sanctuary of peace and tranquility, Mifflins Heritage inn and Tea Room. The owner Dean put us up for the night and treated us to some delicious food (pictured). Although short, our time in Bonavista was delightful. We hope to return soon. Thanks Dean!!

Day 47: Bonavista - Melrose (35km) Strong SW winds let us paddle to Elliston but not much further. We spent a couple hours at the site of the world’s largest puffin chair as we waited for the wind to change. In the meantime, the owner of the gift shop next door showed us how he makes intricate whalebone/moose antler carvings and we learnt about puffins. The wind changed at 6 and propelled us to Melrose where we were offered a shed to crash in. Dean brought us dinner and we feasted.

Day 48: Melrose - TBD we are fuelled up and stoked to knock down the last 100km of the trip. We’re now only Trinity Bay, Conception Bay, and Cape St Francis away from Quidi Vidi.
