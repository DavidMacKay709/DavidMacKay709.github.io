Day 49: We are starting out from Bay De Verde this morning with the goal of reaching Quidi Vidi. If the wind cooperates then we should be able to cover the 56km by 6pm but we’ll keep you posted. Stoked to be on the final stretch but also… confused?

Setting out across Conception Bay for our final crossing, we are looking forward to taking a break from long and committing paddling days but anticipate that once the trip is over we will end up missing at least some aspects of them (but still cant say which). One thing we are incredibly grateful for and are certain we will miss is the warm generosity of the people we’ve encountered along the way and the steadfast support from everyone that has followed along.

Feel free to come meet us [@thewharfnl](https://www.instagram.com/thewharfnl/) this evening for a beer and/or chat (but look for updates on our story in case we’re behind schedule)!
