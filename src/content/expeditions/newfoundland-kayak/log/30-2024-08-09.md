**We’ve made it! “Around The Rock”**

A few days ago we landed back in Quidi Vidi, the same spot we departed from 49 days previous.

Our final day of paddling was perhaps the hardest, fighting strong headwinds for 14 hours as we covered the final 60km. If it hadn’t been our final day, we wouldn’t have pushed on. To say we were elated to arrive would be an understatement.

We have paddled roughly 2200 km, enjoying the best and worst conditions that Newfoundland has to offer.

Now as we sit here writing the post, an immense sense of relief and gratitude continues to set it. It’s hard to find words to describe how we feel, hopefully they will come soon.

To our family, old friends, and the new friends we’ve met along the way, Thank you!
