As students, spending a month and a half of the summer work season on a personal expedition is a financial burden, one that might have deterred us from attempting the trip if it wasn’t for all the support we received. Therefore, we would like to thank our beloved sponsors for outfitting us with the best gear around and making this trip possible.

[@seakayakinguk](https://www.instagram.com/seakayakinguk/) provided us with 2 Explorer Sea Kayaks and 4 4-piece carbon fibre Celtic paddles that we couldnt have been happier with. The boats are stable at rest (enough to acrobatically take bathroom breaks from) and only gained stability with speed. Their steady and predictable behaviour in rough waters allows for uninterrupted paddle strokes, even in confused, reverberating waters off of steep headlands. They can surf straight down swell with blinding speed while remaining maneuverable and deliver a confidence inspiring paddling experience when faced with quartering conditions, as they track straight. The cockpit is comfortable too, from the contoured seat (which we supplemented with foam) to the adjustable foot blocks. The slender 17’ boats carry speed easily through the water and their sturdy foot pegs allow for effective power delivery to the boat with every stroke.

[@marcogroup](https://www.instagram.com/marcogroup/) group, a local NL construction company, showed early faith in this trip and were our first sponsor. Their funding helped us acquire essential safety gear and food.

[@jackpinepaddle](https://www.instagram.com/jackpinepaddle/), a wilderness adventure company owned and operated in Jack’s hometown of Yellowknife, also funded the trip. Jack has worked as a guide at JPP for 5 seasons but spent most of this summer in Newfoundland. We’re incredibly grateful to owner Dan Wong for his support and willingness to part with his best guide for a season.
