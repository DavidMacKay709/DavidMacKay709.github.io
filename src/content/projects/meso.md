MESO (Model for Energy Systems Optimization) is Angler Solutions' software platform for modelling and optimizing hybrid, diversified energy systems. It's used to evaluate how mixes of generation and storage technologies would perform, including the diesel systems that supply many isolated and remote communities.

I'm an Energy Systems E.I.T. at Angler, where I help lead development of MESO's optimization module.
