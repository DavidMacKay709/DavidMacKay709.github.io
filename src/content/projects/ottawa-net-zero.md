I was the integration team lead for a multidisciplinary team of more than 25 students.

- Developed methods to project Ottawa's electric vehicle energy demand from 2023 through 2050
- Built a least-cost investment planning model in MATLAB/Simulink, incorporating wind, nuclear, solar, and load-side efficiency measures to reach net zero by 2050
- Ran sensitivity analyses on the economic impact of different future scenarios: carbon tax, population growth, EV adoption, and efficiency measures
