I worked alongside Professional Engineers and technicians to build MATLAB/Simulink power system models for remote community grids.

## Field work

I surveyed and documented community power system infrastructure from generator to load. That meant recording every component (lines, transformers, switches, fuses) along with its coordinates. We then used the data to build each community's MATLAB/Simulink model.

## Analysis

- Used the models for power system impact studies, assessing how new generation interconnections and new loads would affect isolated grids
- Applied graph theory to analyze the topology of community power systems and identify vulnerabilities

This was the work that moved me from aerospace into power and energy systems.
